# Back up and recover

Your **recovery phrase** (also called a seed phrase) is the master key to every account created from it. With it, you can restore your wallet on any device. Without it, nobody can — not even Amero X.

## Keeping your phrase safe

- Write it on paper, in order. Consider a second copy in a separate safe place.
- Never type it into a website, and never share it with anyone — including anyone claiming to be support.
- Don't photograph it, screenshot it, email it or store it in a notes or cloud app.

## View your recovery phrase again

Open **Settings → Reveal recovery phrase**. Amerox shows a warning first, then asks for your password — every time, even if the wallet is already unlocked — and the words stay blurred until you choose to show them.

If you copy the phrase, Amerox warns you first — other apps on your computer can read the clipboard — and clears the clipboard again shortly afterwards.

## Forgot your password?

Your password only unlocks the wallet on this device. If you forget it, choose **Forgot password?** on the unlock screen. You have two options:

**Restore from recovery phrase.** Enter your recovery phrase and choose a new password. Your accounts come back.

**Erase wallet and start over.** Deletes the wallet from this browser. Only do this if you have your recovery phrase, or the wallet has no funds.

<Callout type="warn" title="Imported private keys are not in your phrase">
Accounts you added by importing a private key, or from a *different* recovery phrase, are not covered by your main recovery phrase. Keep those keys and phrases backed up separately — restoring from your main phrase won't bring them back.
</Callout>

## Moving to a new computer

1. Install Amerox on the new browser.
2. Choose **Import an existing wallet** and enter your recovery phrase.
3. Re-add any extra accounts from **Accounts**, and re-import any private keys.
