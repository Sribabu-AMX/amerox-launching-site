# Install Amerox

Amerox comes in two versions, and you can use either or both with the same recovery phrase:

| | Chrome extension | Android app |
|---|---|---|
| Get it from | Chrome Web Store | Google Play |
| Runs on | Chrome 111+ and other Chromium browsers | Android 7.0 and newer |
| Where | Every country the Chrome Web Store serves | United States, United Kingdom, Nigeria and Ukraine — more countries later |
| Networks | Amero X, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain | The same six, **plus Tron** (TRX and USDT TRC-20) |
| Also | Send and receive, swap, Buy AMX, connect to dapps | All of that, plus moving USDT between networks, a built-in dapp browser and WalletConnect |

<Callout type="warn" title="Only install Amerox from the official store links">
Use the store buttons on the Amerox home page. Search results and ads can point to look-alike apps and extensions built to steal recovery phrases.
</Callout>

## Chrome extension

Amerox runs in **Google Chrome 111 or newer** and in other Chromium-based browsers that install extensions from the Chrome Web Store, such as Brave.

<Steps>

### Open the Chrome Web Store listing

Use the **Add to Chrome** button on the Amerox home page, or [open the listing here](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/canimdcopjlodioinbejamfelpijmblm).

### Add it to your browser

Click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension**. Chrome shows the permissions Amerox asks for (see below).

### Pin it

Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin **Amerox**, so the gold coin is always one click away.

### Create or import a wallet

Click the coin, then **Get started**. Setup opens in its own browser tab — continue with [Create or import a wallet](/docs/create-wallet).

</Steps>

### What the extension asks your browser for

Chrome lists these when you install. Amerox keeps them as small as it can:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Storage | Keeps your encrypted wallet, settings and connected sites on this device. |
| Alarms | Locks the wallet on time, even after the browser has put the extension to sleep. |
| Read and change all your data on all websites | Lets dapps find the wallet — every browser wallet needs this. Amerox only answers a page's requests; it doesn't read or change page content, and nothing happens without your approval. |
| Network access | Only to the network endpoints the wallet uses. A custom network's endpoint is requested separately, when you add it. |

Amerox does not ask for your browsing history, your tabs, or notifications.

## Android app

Amerox for Android is on Google Play in the **United States, the United Kingdom, Nigeria and Ukraine**. More countries will follow — if the listing doesn't open for you, it isn't offered in your country yet.

<Steps>

### Open the Google Play listing

Use the **Get it on Google Play** badge on the Amerox home page, on your phone.

### Install it

Tap **Install**. The app needs Android 7.0 or newer.

### Create or import a wallet

Open Amerox and create a new wallet, or import one with its recovery phrase or a private key. Set a password — it encrypts the wallet on your phone. You can turn on fingerprint or face unlock afterwards, if your phone supports it.

</Steps>

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code. Screens that show your recovery phrase or private key can't be captured in screenshots or screen recordings.

<Callout type="info" title="Tron and the USDT bridge are Android-only for now">
The Android app includes Tron (TRX and USDT TRC-20) and moving USDT between networks. The Chrome extension doesn't have them yet.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Amerox never asks for your recovery phrase on a website">
The only place you ever type your recovery phrase is inside Amerox itself — when you import a wallet or recover it. A site, a pop-up, an email or a "support agent" asking for it is a scam.
</Callout>

## Updating

Chrome updates extensions automatically; Google Play updates apps automatically unless you turned that off. To check your version, open Amerox → **Settings**.

## Removing Amerox

Removing the extension or uninstalling the app deletes the encrypted wallet from that device. Your funds stay on the blockchain and you can restore them anywhere with your recovery phrase — **make sure you have it before you remove Amerox.**
