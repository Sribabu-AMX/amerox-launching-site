# Amerox documentation

Amerox is a self-custody wallet for **Amero X Network** and the EVM chains you already use. It runs as a Chrome extension and as an Android app on Google Play.

Self-custody means your keys are created on your device and never leave it. There is no Amerox account, no email and no server holding your funds — which also means nobody, including Amero X, can recover your wallet for you. Your **recovery phrase** is the only way back in.

## Building a dapp

Amerox is a standard [EIP-1193](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1193) provider and announces itself through [EIP-6963](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6963) with the reverse-DNS name `io.amerox.wallet`. If your dapp already supports injected wallets through wagmi, viem, ethers or a modern connect kit, Amerox works without changes.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Platforms | Chrome 111+ and other Chromium browsers; Android 7.0+ on Google Play (US, UK, Nigeria and Ukraine for now) |
| Built-in networks | Amero X Network, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain — plus Tron in the Android app only |
| Custom networks | Any EVM network you add |
| Recovery phrase | 12 or 24 words (BIP-39) |
| Vault encryption | Argon2id key derivation, XChaCha20-Poly1305 |
| Dapp standards | EIP-1193, EIP-6963, EIP-712 (typed data v3 and v4) |
