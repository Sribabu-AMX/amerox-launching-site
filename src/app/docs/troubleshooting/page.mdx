# Troubleshooting

## My balance is missing after importing a wallet

- Check you're on the right **network** — balances are per network.
- Your phrase may use a different **address layout** (for example, it came from Ledger Live). See [Accounts → Address layout](/docs/accounts#address-layout).
- The account may not be the first one. Add more from **Accounts → Add account → Create a new account**.

## A dapp doesn't show Amerox

- Make sure Amerox is installed, enabled and unlocked, then reload the dapp.
- Look for **Amerox** in the dapp's wallet list. If the dapp only shows a generic "Browser wallet" or "Injected" option and another wallet is also installed, that option may belong to the other wallet — Amerox doesn't take over another wallet's place. Dapps that support EIP-6963 list both by name.

## A request window didn't open, or closed

Requests wait up to five minutes for your answer. If the window was closed or the wallet locked, the request is rejected and the dapp can ask again. Click the Amerox coin to open the wallet if you're asked to unlock.

## My transaction is pending for a long time

The network may be busy, or the fee was low for current conditions. Check the transaction on the block explorer from **Activity**. Most pending transactions confirm once network congestion drops.

## My swap failed or said "no route"

- There may not be enough liquidity for that pair or amount on this network — try a smaller amount or a more common token.
- If the price moved beyond your slippage tolerance, the swap cancels itself and your tokens stay put. Refresh the quote and try again.

## My AMX hasn't arrived after Buy AMX

Delivery happens on Amero X Network after your payment confirms on BNB Smart Chain, and usually takes a few minutes. Amerox shows the status. If it's taking much longer, email [support@amerox.io](mailto:support@amerox.io) with the payment transaction hash from **Activity**.

## I forgot my password

Use **Forgot password?** on the unlock screen and restore from your recovery phrase. See [Back up and recover](/docs/backup#forgot-your-password).

## Still stuck?

Email [support@amerox.io](mailto:support@amerox.io) or reach us on [Telegram](https://t.me/AmeroXSupport). We will **never** ask for your recovery phrase, private key or password.
